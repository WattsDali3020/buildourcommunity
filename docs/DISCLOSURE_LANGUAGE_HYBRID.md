# Hybrid Model Disclosure Language

**Status:** Locked for use  
**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Source:** docs/HYBRID_TOKEN_DESIGN_DECISION.md

This language is required in PPM materials, platform risk disclosures, offering pages, and any public description of token rights.

---

## Core Disclosure Block (Use Verbatim or with Minimal Adaptation)

These tokens represent fractional membership interests (or beneficial rights) in a special-purpose vehicle that holds legal title to real property. They are securities.

**Ownership is designed to be long-term.** The primary purpose of the tokens is to give holders voice (voting and nomination rights) and economic participation in community-driven revitalization projects, not short-term trading.

**Transfers are restricted.** Secondary transfers are permitted only when both the sender and recipient satisfy all applicable eligibility, KYC/AML, sanctions, and offering-rule requirements enforced by the token’s compliance controls. There is no free or unrestricted secondary market by default.

**Liquidity is limited and not guaranteed.** You should be prepared to hold the tokens for an extended period. The ability to sell or transfer may be delayed, restricted, or unavailable depending on compliance status, lock-up periods, community or offering thresholds, and market conditions. Do not invest money you cannot afford to lock up.

**This is not a synthetic or price-tracking product.** Token holders receive the legal and economic rights defined in the operating agreement and offering documents. The token is the official record of those rights.

---

## Short UI Version (Risk Acknowledgment / Purchase Gate)

"These interests are designed for long-term community ownership and governance. Transfers are permissioned and subject to compliance rules. Liquidity is not guaranteed. By continuing you acknowledge that you understand the restricted nature of these tokens and the illiquidity risk."

---

## Messaging Guardrails

- Never describe the tokens as "freely tradable" or "liquid."
- Never lead with secondary market potential.
- Always pair any mention of ownership with the gated-transfer and illiquidity reality.
- Primary activity language should emphasize voting, demand signals, PhaseManager progress, and real-world construction outcomes.

Reg must review any material deviation from this language before public use.
