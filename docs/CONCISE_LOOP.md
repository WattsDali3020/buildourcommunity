# Concise Loop (Phase 0)

The public product is five stocks, four flows, and one valve. Everything else is a coupling document or a founder desk.

## Stocks

1. Demand on a place
2. Mix quality of that demand
3. Threshold progress toward 75%
4. Committed capital (KYC’d tickets → escrow)
5. Light attractiveness (jobs / housing / service vs housing-only)

## Flows

- Nominate or support
- Owner consents to tokenize
- KYC’d ticket into escrow
- Share when momentum is dying

## Valve

PhaseManager at 75%. Nothing important happens before it. Do not claim live county GDP impact until escrow actually moves.

## Seed / map gate

A priority may be **featured** only if its mix is one of:

- `jobs` — light industrial / trades / employment
- `missing_middle` — workforce or missing-middle housing **with** a jobs or service link
- `mixed_use` — downtown mixed-use
- `service` — neighborhood service node

`housing_only` is saved as **parked**. It does not feature on the map. Cherokee is job-thin relative to housing demand (locked readout: high RValve, thin local C/I).

## Public UI rule

Every pin answers four questions in ten seconds:

1. What does this place want?
2. Is the mix right?
3. How close to forming (votes to 75%)?
4. What does $12.50 do if it forms (~$Y toward escrow)?

## Public nav

Map / Explore, Nominate, Learn, Dashboard. Command, Demand, AI Insights, Business, Grants, and RevitaScore stay routed but are not primary nav.

## Do not retune here

- PhaseManager 75%
- Escrow impact-gated 1%
- Contract bytecode

Revisit thresholds only after live participation data exists.
