## Cherokee County / Piedmont Constraints (Current)

- Title and recording remain fully off-chain and local. Token is record of membership interest in SPV, not a substitute for the deed.
- Tax-sale and distressed inventory exist but require careful due diligence; not all inventory is suitable for community-driven tokenization.
- Local economic development and zoning realities constrain what PhaseManager can responsibly advance.
- Construction capacity, permitting timelines, and local contractor availability are binding constraints on impact claims.
- Trust with title companies, local counsel, and county officials is a prerequisite for any credible pilot. This trust is not created by smart contracts.
