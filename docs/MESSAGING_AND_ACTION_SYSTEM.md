# Messaging and Action System

**Status:** Mandatory  
**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Primary Directive Alignment:** Required

Diagnosis and frustration are banned as the primary voice. Every public message must invite a specific, platform-supported action that advances ownership or voice.

---

## Banned Patterns

- “Nobody is making this work for everyone”
- “How many more years will it take”
- “I’ll take an investor”
- “Meme junk / 17 years of nothing”
- Pure diagnosis of the system without a mechanism or invitation

These patterns train the audience to remain spectators.

---

## Required Pattern

Every post or public statement must contain at least one of:

1. A concrete mechanism the platform actually supports, **and**
2. A specific next action a stranger can take (nominate, vote, join waitlist, view a live demand signal, review gated ownership terms).

---

## Approved Message Templates

“Non-owners can nominate properties and vote on what should be built. Owners see that demand and can choose to tokenize under gated, compliant terms. Transfers stay permissioned for the life of the interest. Primary activity is voting and demand, not trading. Start here: [link]”

**Template B — Local Voice**  
“County-weighted voting gives stronger voice to people who live with the outcomes. Demand meters and phase thresholds advance only when real participation is present. If you want a say in what gets built in your community, the nomination flow is open.”

**Template C — Hybrid Reality**  
“These interests are designed for long-term community ownership and governance. Transfers are permissioned and subject to compliance rules. Liquidity is not guaranteed. The product is voice and ownership, not a trading venue. Review the terms and current demand signals: [link]”

**Template D — Pipeline**  
“A property moves from community nomination → owner consent → tokenization only when both demand and legal readiness are present. Track live demand and nominate: [link]”
