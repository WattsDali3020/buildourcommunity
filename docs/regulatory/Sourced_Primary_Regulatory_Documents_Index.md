# Sourced Primary Regulatory Documents Index

This index provides direct links to the official primary source documents for the prioritized regulations relevant to tokenized real estate, fractional ownership, and compliance architecture (based on Nicki Sanders’ notes and RevitaHub’s model).

## 1. Howey Test (Definition of Investment Contract / Security)

**Primary Source:**
- **SEC v. W.J. Howey Co., 328 U.S. 293 (1946)**  
  Full Supreme Court opinion: https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/328/293/

**Key Modern Application:**
- **SEC Framework for “Investment Contract” Analysis of Digital Assets** (April 3, 2019)  
  Official SEC page: https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/framework-investment-contract-analysis-digital-assets

## 3. Regulation A+ (Reg A+)

**Primary Sources:**
- **17 CFR § 230.251 – 230.263** (Regulation A)  
  eCFR: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-230/subject-group-ECFR68d879261fb42fb

## 4. Broker-Dealer Registration Requirements

**Primary Sources:**
- **Securities Exchange Act of 1934, Section 15(a)** – Registration of brokers and dealers  
  Official text: https://www.sec.gov/about/laws/sea34.pdf (full Act) or https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/78o
- **SEC Rule 15a-6** (foreign broker exemption)
- **FINRA Registration resources**: https://www.finra.org/registration-exams-ce/broker-dealer-registration

---

## 5. Transfer Agent Requirements

**Primary Sources:**
- **Securities Exchange Act of 1934, Section 17A**
- **SEC Rules 17Ad-1 through 17Ad-21** (Transfer Agent Rules)  
  Available in 17 CFR Part 240: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240
- **Form TA-1** (Application for Registration as Transfer Agent) and **Form TA-2** (Annual Report)

---

## 6. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) / Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) + Virtual Assets

**Primary Sources:**
- **Bank Secrecy Act (BSA)** as amended: https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-and-regulations/bank-secrecy-act
- **FinCEN Guidance on Virtual Assets and Virtual Asset Service Providers** (multiple advisories, especially 2019–2023 updates)
  - Key 2019 guidance: https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-and-regulations/guidance
- **FinCEN Travel Rule** (31 CFR 1010.410(f) and related)  
  Official FinCEN page on Travel Rule: https://www.fincen.gov/travel-rule

---

## 7. Investment Company Act of 1940 – Exemptions

**Primary Sources:**
- **Investment Company Act of 1940, Section 3(c)(1)** and **Section 3(c)(7)**  
  Full Act: https://www.sec.gov/about/laws/ica40.pdf
- **SEC guidance on private fund exemptions**: https://www.sec.gov/investment-management

---

## 8. State Securities Laws (“Blue Sky Laws”) & Preemption

**Primary Sources:**
- **National Securities Markets Improvement Act of 1996 (NSMIA)** – Preemption provisions (especially for covered securities under Reg A+ Tier 2)
- SEC summary of state preemption: Available via SEC Regulation A resources above
- Individual state securities division websites (for specific qualification requirements under Reg D)
